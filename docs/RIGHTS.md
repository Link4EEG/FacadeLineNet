# Rights and attribution

Research authors: Ju Hyun Lee, Michael J. Ostwald and Ji Seung Yeul.
Participating institutions: UNSW Sydney and Hanyang University.

The public repository includes the software, model checkpoints, numerical research
results and a newly generated schematic example. The original commercial-source
architectural drawings and expert masks are not included.

The project-specific software and checkpoint redistribution licences are awaiting
author confirmation. Public availability alone does not grant a reuse licence.
Third-party dependencies retain their own licences, including PyTorch,
torchvision, NumPy, SciPy and Pillow. The complete A5 checkpoint contains a model
whose ResNet-34 encoder was initialised from torchvision's ImageNet weights before
task-specific training.

and also has affiliation 2 (Hanyang School of Architecture). The separate
faculty-only UNSW affiliation 3 has been removed. The UNSW profile confirms the
visiting appointment and faculty; the school-level correction is author-confirmed.

professor role; its historical degree department is not used as a current job affiliation.
